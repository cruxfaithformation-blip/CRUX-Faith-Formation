# Pillar Card Image Prompts

Generated images go in this folder, named exactly as listed below.
When ready for deployment, copy to: `CRUX Deploy/images/module-cards/`

**Leonardo.ai settings for all five:**
- Model: Leonardo Kino XL or PhotoReal v2
- Aspect ratio: 2:3 (portrait, card format)
- Guidance scale: 7–9
- Negative prompt: `modern clothing, contemporary setting, anime, cartoon, flat illustration, low quality, blurry, text overlays, watermark, digital art style`
